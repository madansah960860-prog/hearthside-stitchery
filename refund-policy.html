The short version, including the one rule that catches people out

You have 30 days from delivery to send something back, and changing your mind counts. It has to be unused and in its original packaging.

Yarn and fabric have one extra condition: they must be uncut and still in the original band or fold. Once a skein has been wound into a cake, once a ball band has come off, or once a length has been cut from a fabric cut, nobody else can be sold it as new and we cannot take it back. We put that sentence on every yarn and fabric product page, in the header of every page, and in the cart, because it is the one term customers most often meet for the first time when it is already too late.

Anything faulty is returnable however far into it you are, and the uncut rule does not apply.

When the clock starts

The 30 days run from the carrier's delivery date, not from the order date and not from when you opened the parcel. Your request must reach us inside those 30 days; the parcel then has a further 14 days to reach us.

Condition we need things back in

- Unused. Not wound, not cut, not knitted with, not cut into, not clamped to a table. - Complete. Every skein, every cut, the dye-lot card, the pattern card, the case, the liner. - In its original packaging. The bundle ribbon, the bag, the box, the roll. - Undamaged by you. Handling it while you decided is expected. A cut ball band is not.

The outer shipping carton does not need to be perfect — you were meant to open it.

If something reaches us used or incomplete we photograph it, email you, and offer a partial refund reflecting the condition or the item returned to you at our cost. We do not keep it and refuse the refund.

Specifically for yarn and fabric

Returnable :: A full skein with the band intact. An unopened bundle with the ribbon still tied. A fabric cut still folded as supplied, uncut and unwashed. An unopened quilt kit with its seal unbroken. Not returnable :: A skein wound into a cake or a centre-pull ball. A skein with the band removed. Any length cut from a fabric cut, however short. Fabric that has been pre-washed. A quilt kit whose fabric bag has been opened. Returnable anyway, because it is a fault :: A knot in the middle of a skein. A skein short of its stated yardage. Fibre content that does not match the label. A dye fault or a colour difference between skeins sold as one lot. A fabric cut short of its stated size. A flaw, hole or run in the cloth.

If you are not sure whether opening something will cost you the right to return it, telephone (717) 555-0119 before you open it and ask. We would much rather have that conversation first.

Other things we cannot take back

- Quilt kits with the fabric bag opened. The kit is cut to a list; once it is opened we cannot certify it is complete. - Items marked final sale. There are none at present. If there ever are, the product page will say so in plain words above the Add to Cart button.

How to start a return

1. Email stitches@hearthsidestitchery.com with Return in the subject line, inside 30 days of delivery. Or telephone (717) 555-0119 during Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00am to 5:00pm Eastern Time and we will do it on the call. 2. Tell us your order number (of the form HS-30271, in your confirmation email), which item, how many, and whether it is faulty or a change of mind — with a photograph if it is faulty. 3. We reply within one business day with a Returns Number, the return address, and a prepaid label as a PDF where the fault is ours. 4. Write the Returns Number on the outside. Pack the item in its own packaging inside an outer carton, and send it tracked; we cannot refund a parcel that never arrives. 5. Send us the tracking number. We will confirm when it lands.

Please do not send anything back without a Returns Number. Our unit also takes in bolt and mill deliveries, and unlabelled parcels get lost among them.

Who pays the postage

We pay, and we refund your original shipping as well :: It arrived damaged. It is faulty. A skein is knotted, short or mislabelled. A fabric cut is short. We sent the wrong thing. The item is materially different from the description here. You pay, and we refund the price of the goods :: You changed your mind. The colour was not what you pictured on your screen. You ordered two bundles and needed one. The yardage was correctly stated and turned out to be more than you needed.

A change-of-mind return usually costs $7 to $12 for yarn, books and notions, and $18 to $28 for the mat set, the quilt kit or the basket, paid by you directly to the carrier. We neither mark it up nor take a cut.

If your order qualified for free shipping over $45 and returning part of it drops the kept portion below $45, we will not go back and charge you the $5.95 afterwards.

Exchanges

We do not run a formal exchange. With sixteen products it is slower than simply placing a new order and returning the first under this policy — that way the replacement is already travelling while the original comes back.

Where the exchange is needed because we sent the wrong thing, none of that applies: the correct item goes straight out, you order nothing and you pay nothing.

Faults and what is not a fault

Report transit damage within 14 days of delivery with photographs. For a fault found in use, tell us as soon as it appears and within the 30 days. We look at photographs, ask what happened, and replace or refund at our cost where it is a manufacturing fault.

Some things are not faults, and it is fairer to name them now. Not-superwash yarn felted in a washing machine. A cutting mat warped after being stored upright or left near a radiator. Fabric shears blunted by cutting paper. A willow basket cracked after a year in a dry heated room with no misting. Soft-grip hook handles deformed after being left in a hot car. Every one of those is written as a care instruction on the product page, and following those instructions is what keeps the item covered.

Natural variation is also not a fault: hand-woven baskets vary in weave and diameter, and dye lots differ between separate purchases, which is exactly why we sell bundles from one lot.

Cancelling

Before it ships, telephone (717) 555-0119 or email stitches@hearthsidestitchery.com with the order number. We cancel and refund in full within 7 working days, and we do not ask why.

After a tracking number exists it becomes a return. You can refuse delivery — it comes back and we refund the goods — but refused parcels take two to three weeks to return, so emailing is faster.

If we cannot agree

Email stitches@hearthsidestitchery.com with Escalation in the subject line and the order number. An owner will read it personally and reply within two business days.

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under Pennsylvania consumer law or the consumer protection law of your own state.
