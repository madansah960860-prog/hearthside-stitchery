What this notice is

This explains how Hearthside Stitch & Supply, Inc., trading as Hearthside Stitchery, handles personal information taken through hearthsidestitchery.com, through our order emails, through the seasonal letter, and over the telephone on (717) 555-0119.

We are a three-person shop. We are not an advertising business, and this site carries no analytics tag, no advertising pixel, no social embed and no third-party script whatsoever. That is a property of the code you can confirm by viewing the page source, not a promise about our intentions.

The information we hold

### What you give us Order information :: Name, delivery address, billing address, email address, telephone number. Required to cut, pack, address and send an order. Payment information :: Entered on the payment processor's own hosted page. Your full card number never reaches our servers and is never stored by us. We see only the last four digits, the card brand, the authorisation outcome and the amount. Messages :: Whatever is in the contact form or an email to us: name, email address, order number if given, and the message. Seasonal letter :: Email address and the date you joined. Telephone orders :: The same order information, taken by hand and then entered in the order system. Tuesday class enquiries :: Name and a contact number or email, kept only until the term you asked about has finished.

What is collected without you typing it

Our web host keeps standard server logs: requesting IP address, timestamp, file requested, HTTP status, referring page and browser user-agent. They exist for security and diagnostics, are kept 30 days, and are then deleted.

This site sets no cookies, first-party or third-party. Your cart lives in your browser's localStorage, a browser storage feature rather than a cookie: it stays on your machine, is never transmitted to us, and disappears when you clear your browser data. We cannot see anybody's cart.

Why we hold it

- To cut, pack, address and send your order, and to handle returns and refunds. - To send transactional email: order confirmation, shipping confirmation with tracking, a delay notice if one becomes necessary, and refund confirmations. - To answer your question or telephone you back. - To send the seasonal letter, if you asked and until you ask us to stop. - To keep the records tax and accounting law require. - To spot and prevent fraudulent orders.

We do not build advertising profiles, we do not profile customers, and we make no automated decisions about anyone.

Cookies

There is no cookie banner because there are no cookies. Nothing here tracks you.

Two browser features are used, both on your own device: localStorage for the cart, and the browser's ordinary HTTP cache for our stylesheet and images.

Global Privacy Control signals and Do Not Track headers are honoured by default, in the simple sense that there is no cross-context tracking here for them to disable.

Who else sees it

Only the service providers needed to actually send a parcel, each engaged as a service provider or processor, each permitted to use the information only for that job and never for their own purposes.

Payment processor
Name, billing address, email and card details, taken directly from you on its own hosted page, to take payment and issue refunds.
Carriers, USPS and UPS
Name, delivery address, telephone number and parcel weight, to deliver and to provide tracking.
Email provider
Email address and the content of transactional email and the seasonal letter.
Web host
The site files and the 30-day server logs described above.
Accountant and tax authorities
Order and payment records, as the law requires.

We will also disclose where legally compelled — a valid subpoena, court order or lawful demand — and we will tell you where we are permitted to. If the business were sold, customer records could pass to the buyer; we would email everyone affected beforehand.

We have never sold personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Not for anybody, and not for anybody under 16.

Rights you can exercise

Several states grant privacy rights to their residents. We honour all of the following for every customer rather than checking your address first.

  • To know — the categories and specific pieces we hold, their sources, our purposes, and who we have disclosed them to.
  • To delete — everything except the records we are obliged to retain for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes.
  • To correct — anything inaccurate.
  • To opt out of sale or sharing — there is nothing to opt out of. That is why there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link here: the practice it would switch off does not occur.
  • To limit use of sensitive personal information — we collect none as the statutes define it: no Social Security number, no financial account number, no precise geolocation, no biometric, genetic or health data.
  • Not to be discriminated against — exercising a right will never change your price, your service, or whether we accept your order.
  • To appeal — if we refuse, we explain why and how to appeal, and an owner reviews it.

These apply to California residents under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and on substantially similar terms to residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy laws.

Making a request

Email stitches@hearthsidestitchery.com with Privacy request in the subject line, or telephone (717) 555-0119 during Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00am to 5:00pm Eastern Time. Say which right you are exercising and give the email address or order number you used so we can find your records.

We acknowledge within 10 days and answer substantively within 45 days. Where we genuinely need longer we tell you inside those 45 days and take up to a further 45, never more than 90 days in total. An authorised agent will be asked for written authority, and we will verify with you directly.

There is no charge. A manifestly unfounded or excessive request could attract a reasonable fee, but we would explain and agree it with you first.

How long we keep it

Order records :: 7 years, as tax and accounting rules require. Payment records (last four digits only) :: 7 years, with the order. Contact messages and email :: 24 months from the last message, then deleted. Seasonal letter address :: Until you unsubscribe, then removed within 10 days. Class enquiries :: Until the end of the term enquired about. Server logs :: 30 days. Returns and refunds :: 7 years, with the order.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and us is encrypted. Card details are entered on the processor's own PCI-DSS compliant page and never touch our systems. Order records are accessible only to the three of us, each with a separate account and multi-factor authentication. Paper records are locked.

No system is perfectly secure and we will not claim otherwise. If a breach affected your personal information we would notify you and the relevant state authorities without undue delay, as state breach-notification law requires.

Children's privacy

This is a general-audience craft supply shop. It is not directed to children under 13, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and nothing here is designed to appeal particularly to children.

Several products contain sharp tools and are sold for adult use; that is stated on the relevant product pages. If we learn we hold information from a child under 13 we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has given us information, email stitches@hearthsidestitchery.com and we will remove it and write back to confirm.

Ordering requires you to be at least 18 — see the terms of service.

Changes

The in-force date at the top always shows the current version. For a change that materially affects your rights we will email the seasonal letter list and put a notice on the home page for 30 days, and we will not apply a materially worse policy retroactively to information already held.
