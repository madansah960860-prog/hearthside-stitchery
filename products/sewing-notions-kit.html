About this one

Every one of these costs almost nothing on its own and every one of them stops a project dead when you cannot find it. Buying them as a set is not about value, it is about having them all in the same place.

In the case: a tape measure, a seam ripper, small embroidery scissors, a needle threader, a pack of assorted hand-sewing needles, a pack of glass-head pins, a magnetic pin dish, a thimble, six reels of polyester thread in useful colours, a tailor's chalk wedge, a bodkin, twenty stitch markers, a row counter, a pack of safety pins and a small pair of thread snips.

The case is a zipped fabric wallet, 9 by 6 inches, with elastic loops and two mesh pockets so everything has a slot and you can see at a glance what is missing.

The scissors, the seam ripper, the pins and the needles are genuinely sharp. This set is not a toy and should be kept away from children.
