About this one

Prints get the attention and solids do the work. A quilt made entirely of prints has nowhere for the eye to rest, and the usual fix is to put a solid between them. This stack gives you ten, graded from pale through mid-tone to deep, so you can shade a design rather than just alternate two colours.

Each cut is a full half-yard off a 44-inch bolt, so 18 by 44 inches, which is twice what a fat quarter gives you and the right amount for sashing and borders rather than patches.

Same cloth quality as our print bundle — roughly 145 grams per square metre, tightly woven, 44-inch goods. Solids show a seam and a press more honestly than prints do, which is another reason to use good cloth for them.

Ten half-yards is 5 yards of fabric in total: enough for the sashing, borders and binding of a lap or twin quilt, with a good deal left for the next one.
