About this one

This is the set to buy for somebody who has said they might like to try knitting. It contains exactly enough to make one scarf and nothing else to decide about, which is the main reason beginners give up before they start.

In the box: one 100-gram ball of a soft pastel acrylic and wool blend, 220 yards, worsted weight; one pair of 10-inch US 8 bamboo needles; a blunt darning needle for sewing the ends in; and a single printed pattern for a garter-stitch scarf with the cast-on and cast-off drawn step by step rather than described.

The blend is 70 per cent acrylic to 30 per cent wool. That is deliberate for a first project: it is machine washable, it costs little enough that pulling the whole thing out twice does not matter, and it still has enough wool in it to feel like yarn rather than string.

Two hundred and twenty yards makes a scarf roughly 6 inches by 60 inches in garter stitch.
