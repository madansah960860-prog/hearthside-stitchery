About this one

Bamboo needles are the ones to own if you knit loosely or if metal needles feel slippery. The surface has just enough grip that a stitch will sit on the shaft instead of sliding off the end while you reposition your hands, and they are warm to hold, which matters more than it sounds over a long evening.

Nine pairs, 14-inch single-pointed, in US sizes 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 10.5 and 13 (3.5 mm to 9.0 mm). Each size is laser-marked on the cap, not printed, so the marking does not wear off with use — a genuinely common annoyance with cheaper sets.

The bamboo is carbonised, which is a heat treatment that hardens the fibre and darkens it; it makes the smaller sizes much less prone to bending than pale untreated bamboo. Everything comes in a cotton canvas roll with a numbered slot per pair and a tie closure.

Bamboo is a natural material. It will develop a polish from your hands over the years, which is a feature, and it will snap if you sit on it, which is not.

  • Care

    • Wipe with a dry cloth. Do not wash bamboo needles or soak them.
    • If a shaft ever feels rough, smooth it with 600-grit paper and rub a little beeswax in.
    • Store rolled in the case rather than loose in a bag; loose bamboo needles get stepped on.
    • Keep away from radiators and direct sun, which dry the bamboo out and make it brittle.
