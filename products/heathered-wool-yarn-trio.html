About this one

A heathered yarn is spun from fibres dyed separately and then blended, so the colour has depth in it rather than sitting flat. It is very forgiving: cabling, texture and ribbing all read clearly against a heather in a way they can disappear against a solid.

This is 80 per cent wool and 20 per cent nylon in aran weight, 100 grams and 180 yards a ball, three balls to the pack. The nylon is there for abrasion resistance rather than to pad the price out — it is what makes this a sensible yarn for a cardigan's elbows or a pair of slippers.

Three balls is 540 yards: an adult hat and matching mittens, a long scarf, or the yoke of a sweater. The oatmeal shade is deliberately neutral so it pairs with almost anything you already have in a basket.
