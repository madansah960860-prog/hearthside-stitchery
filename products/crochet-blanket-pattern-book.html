About this one

Crochet patterns come in two dialects — written instructions and symbol charts — and most people can read one but not the other. Every pattern in this book is printed both ways on facing pages, so you never have to learn the other dialect to use the book.

Twenty-four designs, sorted into three sections of eight: granny-square and motif blankets, textured all-over stitch blankets, and ripple and chevron blankets. Each one lists the exact yarn weight and total yardage, the hook size, the finished measurements, and the gauge swatch to work before you start.

It is 160 pages, spiral-bound so it lies flat next to you while you work, printed on 120gsm matt paper with a photograph of every finished blanket at a size where you can actually see the stitch. The stitch charts are set at 14 point, which is larger than most books manage.

There is a 16-page reference section at the front covering every stitch used, with step-by-step photographs, so the book does not assume you already know.
