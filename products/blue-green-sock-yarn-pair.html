About this one

Sock yarn is fingering weight with nylon in it, and the nylon is not optional — a pure wool sock wears through at the heel in a season. This is 75 per cent superwash merino to 25 per cent nylon, which is the standard blend for a reason.

Two skeins, one blue and one green, 100 grams and 420 yards each. One skein makes one pair of adult socks up to about a US men's 11 with yarn to spare, so the pair here is two pairs of socks. Buying them as a two-colour set also gives you contrast heels and toes without a third purchase.

It is superwash, so finished socks go in the machine on a wool cycle. Do not tumble dry them — that is what shortens the life of a hand-knitted sock more than wearing them does.

  • Care

    • Machine wash finished socks on a wool cycle at 30°C / 85°F.
    • Do not tumble dry. Lay flat or hang to dry.
    • Do not bleach.
    • Darn holes early. A small hole in a sock heel becomes a large one in a fortnight.
