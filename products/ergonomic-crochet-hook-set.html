About this one

The handle is the reason to buy this set. A bare aluminium hook is about 0.2 inches across and you hold it with your fingertips; these have a moulded soft-grip handle 0.55 inches across with a flat thumb rest, which lets you hold the hook in your whole hand instead.

"Ergonomic" here is a description of that shape and nothing more. We are not going to tell you what it will do for your hands — that is not a claim a yarn shop is in any position to make. What we can tell you is the handle diameter, the thumb-rest width and the weight, so you can compare it with a hook you already own.

Eight sizes: B/1, C/2, D/3, E/4, F/5, G/6, H/8 and J/10 (2.25 mm to 6.0 mm), each with a different handle colour and the size moulded into the handle as well as stamped on the shaft. The hook heads are inline rather than tapered, which most people find easier to keep an even tension with.

They come in a zipped case with elastic loops, which is what stops a set like this migrating to the bottom of four different bags.

    • Wipe with a damp cloth. Do not put them in a dishwasher — heat degrades the soft grip.
