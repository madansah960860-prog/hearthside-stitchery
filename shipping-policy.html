Where we send parcels

Within the United States and nowhere else: all fifty states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico. Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico pay the same flat rate and reach the free-shipping threshold on the same terms, but ground transit to them takes considerably longer and your confirmation email will say so.

PO boxes and APO, FPO and DPO military addresses are fine and travel by United States Postal Service. Two items are exceptions on size: the Rotary Cutter &amp; Self-Healing Mat Set (24 by 18 inches, sent flat in a rigid mailer) and the Willow Yarn Basket (an oversize carton) both need a street address. If one of those is ordered to a PO box we telephone you rather than cancelling silently.

We do not post outside the United States and we do not post to freight forwarders. A US billing card does not change that.

What it costs

Flat $5.95 per order, regardless of weight, contents or destination. Free once the subtotal reaches $45 before tax. Expedited is a flat $15.95 per order and has no free threshold.

There is no handling charge, no packaging charge, no fuel surcharge and no minimum order. What the cart shows is what you pay, plus sales tax where it applies.

One charge per order
Even where an order travels as two parcels — which happens when a quilt kit and a mat set go out together — you pay one shipping charge and may get two tracking numbers.
Oversize items
The mat set and the willow basket cost us well over $5.95 to send. We absorb that rather than adding an oversize fee at the last step.

How long the two stages take

### Packing time, here - Books, notions kit, starter set: 1 business day - Yarn, needles, hooks, fabric cuts, shears, basket: 1 to 2 business days - Nine-Patch Quilt Kit: 2 to 3 business days, because every kit is checked piece by piece against the cutting list before it is sealed - Rotary Cutter &amp; Mat Set: 2 business days, packed flat in a rigid mailer - Orders placed after 2:00pm Eastern Time, on a Sunday or Monday (we are closed), or on a federal holiday start their clock on the next business day we are open

Transit time, the carrier's

Estimates from the carriers, not guarantees, and not including our packing time.

  • Ground, lower forty-eight: 3 to 6 business days
  • Ground, Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico: 6 to 12 business days
  • Expedited, lower forty-eight: 1 to 3 business days
  • USPS to PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO: 4 to 10 business days, and military addresses can run much longer once inside the military postal system, which nobody can track

In practice, a standard order to a street address in the lower forty-eight arrives four to eight business days after it is placed.

Carriers and tracking

USPS Ground Advantage, USPS Priority Mail, UPS Ground and UPS 2nd Day Air, chosen on size, weight and destination. We do not offer a carrier choice at checkout because for most of our parcels it changes neither the cost nor the arrival day.

Two emails: an order confirmation when you place it, and a shipping confirmation with carrier and tracking number when it leaves. Tracking normally begins moving within a day; a number that has not updated overnight is normal.

No shipping confirmation after four business days means something has gone wrong here. Telephone (717) 555-0119 or email stitches@hearthsidestitchery.com and we will find out what.

If we are going to be late

The Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule sets out what a seller has to do when it cannot ship on time. Here is what we will do, written down rather than implied.

  • We advertise only shipping times we have a reasonable basis to meet. Where no time is stated, we ship within 30 days of receiving a properly completed order.
  • If we will miss the date we gave you, we contact you on or before that date with either a definite revised date or a plain statement that we cannot give one, together with your right to cancel for a full refund at no cost.
  • Where the revised date is 30 days or less after the original, you need not reply — silence counts as agreement to the short delay, and you may still cancel any time before it ships.
  • Where the revised date is more than 30 days after the original, or where no date can be given, the order is cancelled and refunded automatically unless you expressly tell us you will wait.
  • If we then miss the revised date as well, we write again first, and silence is no longer agreement: without your express consent the order is cancelled and refunded.
  • Cancelling is one line. Reply to the notice, telephone (717) 555-0119 or email stitches@hearthsidestitchery.com. We do not ask for a reason.

A delay cancellation is refunded in full to the original payment method within 7 working days.

Damage, loss and short counts

### Damaged in transit Email stitches@hearthsidestitchery.com within 14 days of delivery with the order number and photographs of the item and the outer carton. We replace where we have stock and refund in full where we do not, and we pay the postage either way. Keep the packaging until we reply; the carrier sometimes asks for it.

A skein or a cut is short or faulty

A knot in the middle of a skein, a skein short of its stated yardage, a mislabelled fibre content, a dye fault or a fabric cut short of size is a fault, and the uncut rule in our returns policy does not apply to it. Tell us with a photograph and we replace or refund at our cost, however far into the project you are.

Marked delivered, nothing arrived

Check with everyone at the address and the usual spots first — most turn up within a day. Still missing after three business days, tell us. We open the carrier claim ourselves and, whatever it concludes, you get a replacement or a refund. Chasing carriers is our job.

Tracking stalled

Nothing moving for seven consecutive business days: tell us and we open a lost-in-transit claim. Replacement or refund, your choice.

Wrong item sent

Our mistake. Prepaid label plus the correct item, and you are not out of pocket.

Addresses

Please check the delivery address before ordering. A parcel returned as undeliverable because the address was wrong or incomplete is refunded in full less only the actual return-transit cost the carrier charged us, which we will show you on their invoice. Where the error is ours we resend at our cost.

We cannot change an address after a parcel has shipped. Telephone us before it goes and we can.

Sales tax

Calculated at checkout from your delivery address where applicable and shown as its own line before you confirm. Never folded into the goods price or the shipping charge. As a Pennsylvania business we collect Pennsylvania sales and use tax, and we collect in other states where economic-nexus thresholds require it.
